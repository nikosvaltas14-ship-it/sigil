CIRCUIT FIGURES
- Draw circuits in the style of ΑΠΘ exam papers: current source = circle with
  the arrow INSIDE it; voltage source = explicit +/− polarity marks.
- Source direction and polarity are not cosmetic: they fix the sign/angle of
  derived quantities. Double-check them in every figure.
- In a Norton equivalent, draw Z_Th as a vertical parallel branch.
- Include a «Ανάγνωση Σχήματος» section with its own worked examples and
  tiered exercises: identify the reference node, nodes, branches and loops,
  read each current-source arrow (which node it injects into), each
  voltage-source polarity, spot series/parallel, and write the equations
  directly from the figure.
